# 03 - The framework lens and the confidence gate (Stages 4 and 5)

This is principle 2 in operation, and it is the file most likely to drift into a personality scorer. It must not. The frameworks are how you THINK about the signal log. They are never what you SHOW. Nothing in this file is a scoring rubric. There is no "rate each dimension" step anywhere, because that is the exact thing the method forbids.

The discipline: framework reasoning goes IN, a quoted source-cited qualitative observation comes OUT. The trait name, the value type, the affect label, and any number all stay on the inside.

---

## The three lenses (used to reason, never named in output)

Read the append-only signal log through these to organize your thinking and ask sharper questions of the text:

- **Big Five (OCEAN).** A lens for noticing how a person tends to engage: how open to new things, how orderly, how outward, how warm, how easily unsettled they sound across many signals. Use it to spot a pattern worth quoting, not to score the person.
- **Schwartz basic values.** A lens for what a person treats as worth pursuing: security, achievement, self-direction, benevolence, and so on. Use it to notice which value a cluster of quotes keeps pointing at, then quote the cluster, not the value.
- **Affect.** A lens for the emotional weather: what lifts the person, what flattens them, what recurs. Use it to surface a felt pattern, then quote the feeling in their own words.

In every case the lens is a question-generator. It asks "is there a recurring pull toward self-direction here?" and you answer by going back to the log and finding the quotes that show it, or finding that they do not exist.

---

## The transform: lens reasoning in, quoted observation out

This is the heart of the file. Worked examples, with the internal reasoning shown only to make the transform clear. The internal reasoning never reaches the page.

| Internal reasoning (stays inside, never printed) | What goes on the page (quoted, source-cited, no framework, no number) |
|---|---|
| Values lens: many signals point at self-direction over security. Schwartz value type noted internally. | "You keep coming back to doing things your own way. You wrote, 'I would rather build the wrong thing my way than the right thing someone else's way.'" |
| Affect lens: positive affect spikes only around intensity and pressure; flat otherwise. | "The moments you sound most alive are the loud ones. You said, 'the only time I feel switched on is when something is on fire.'" |
| OCEAN lens: high openness pattern, repeated novelty-seeking across the log. | "New things pull you. Across the month you kept starting fresh threads: 'I want to learn X', then 'actually let me try Y instead'." |
| Upload aggregate: five engagements with genai material in four weeks. | "You keep returning to genai. You opened material on it five times this month." |

Notice what is absent from the right column: no trait name, no value-type name, no affect label, no score, no percentile, no type code. Just the person's own words and a plain observation that sits on top of them.

Rule for the output: if a sentence could only have been written by reading a textbook (it names a trait or a type), cut it. If it could only have been written by reading THIS person's notes (it quotes them), keep it.

---

## The confidence gate (Stage 5)

Confidence decides whether an observation, especially a surprising one, is allowed to surface. It is an internal gate. The number is never displayed.

- **Plain reflections** (a recurring topic the person would recognize) surface freely once supported by the log.
- **Surprises** (a stated-versus-revealed tension, a blind spot, something the person has not noticed) surface only when the evidence is strong, roughly 0.6 confidence and above, held internally. Below that, hold it back. A wrong surprise is worse than a missed one.

How to weigh confidence internally, without printing it:

- Count the recurrence in the append-only log. One quote is thin. The same pattern across many dated signals is strong.
- Check for a real contrast for a tension: you need both sides quoted (the stated thing and the revealed thing), not one side plus your inference.
- Discount anything resting on uploads alone for a personal read, since an upload is only a behavioral signal until it aggregates.

If a signal team or template wants a confidence indicator, band it in words, never in figures: "tentative" versus "well-supported". Never write a number like a confidence of 0.7 about a psychological read. That is the same fabricated-precision trap as a printed trait score.

---

## From a gated surprise to an invitation (principle 3)

A surprise that passes the gate is still never a verdict. It becomes a question. Quote both sides, then open it:

> You say you want a calm year. Your notes keep chasing the next big thing. The calm shows up in June, the next big thing shows up a week later. What is that about?

That is an invitation to reflect. The person can take it or leave it. The phrasing patterns and the verdict-to-question rewrites are in `04-output-format.md`.
