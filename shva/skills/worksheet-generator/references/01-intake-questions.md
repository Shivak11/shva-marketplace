# Stage 1 — Intake Questions

Understand the teaching intent before building. Use **AskUserQuestion**. Batch the questions, give
sensible default options, and let Shiva add free text. Skip any he already answered in his prompt.

Aim for a short, friendly intake. AskUserQuestion caps at **4 questions per call**, so use **1 to 2 calls** (e.g. the first 4 core questions, then the rest), not one giant call and not an interrogation.

## The core questions

1. **What do you want them to walk away able to do?**
   (The one capability or realization. Not a topic, an outcome.)
   - Options to offer: "Make a decision they've been avoiding", "See a system they're inside",
     "Reframe how they think about X", "Other".

2. **What is the single thing at the centre?**
   (Every good worksheet has one unit traced to its end: a decision, a tension, a constraint, a
   workflow, a metric, a habit.)
   - Options: "A specific decision", "A tension / trade-off", "A workflow or process", "A skill or
     habit", "Other".

3. **Who is in the room?**
   (Seniority, function, how much they already know. This sets the example case and the language.)
   - Options: "Senior HR / people leaders", "Mixed senior managers", "Founders / CXOs",
     "Students / early-career", "Other".

4. **Drop your rough ideas / fragments.**
   (Free text. Any phrases, frameworks, examples, or half-thoughts Shiva already has. These seed the
   arc and the worked example. Encourage messiness.)

5. **How long is the slot, and how many filled-in pages feel right?**
   - Options: "~30 min, 2 pages", "~60 min, 3–4 pages", "~90 min, 5–7 pages", "Other".

6. **Deliverable now?**
   - Options: "HTML only (I'll review, then ask for PDF)" — recommended default —, "HTML then PDF
     in one go", "Other".

## Useful follow-ups (ask only if relevant)

- Is there a specific talk, video, book, or wiki note this should lean on? (Feeds the YouTube /
  book-search / wiki research.)
- Should it ship with a companion questioner GPT for the room? (Default yes; adapt
  `decision-decomposer-gpt.md`.)
- Any house example you want threaded through (a real plant, a real role)? Otherwise pick a vivid,
  audience-appropriate one and say which you chose.

## After intake

Restate, in two lines, the spine you heard: *"One <unit> traced from <felt problem> to <consequence>."*
Confirm or adjust, then move to Stage 2 research. Do not start drafting fields yet.
