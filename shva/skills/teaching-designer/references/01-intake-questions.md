# 01 - Discovery questions (Stage 1)

Never build from a one-line brief. Use AskUserQuestion (at most 4 questions per call, 1 to 2 calls). Offer sensible default options and let Shiva add free text. Only ask what the brief has not already answered.

Learn at minimum:

1. **Topic and the single central idea.** What is the one contrarian idea or tension at the centre? If Shiva gives a thesis (for example "AI cross-skills, it does not upskill"), that is the spine. If not, propose one after research.
2. **Audience and seniority.** Senior HR, CXO, faculty, students. This sets how light and how much theory.
3. **Length and format.** 90 minutes, half day, a 9 to 11 session program. Light discussion-led, or hands-on, or lecture.
4. **How light.** Confirm: analogies plus clips plus discussion, names as garnish, no worksheets. Most executive sessions want this.
5. **Deliverable.** Topic recommendation, tabular session plan, teaching script, or the interactive HTML. Default to the plan when ambiguous, and confirm before building the HTML.

After discovery, do not jump to building. Research first (Stage 2), then recommend the scaled-spine structure and let Shiva converge (Stage 3).

## Converge before building (Stage 3 questions)

After proposing the topic structure, ask Shiva to pick:

- The backbone cut: the 3-act scaled spine as is, rebalanced, reordered, or fewer acts.
- The theory weight: names as light garnish, one framework taught, or an academic framing.
- The next deliverable: a verified video and quote pack, the tabular plan, or the full script and HTML.

This converge-before-building gate prevents a heavy build in the wrong direction.
