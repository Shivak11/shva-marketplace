## Editorial invariants

- Start from the statement under the statement: displayed claim, avoided cost or
  action, operative constraint, displaced blame, and the official story that
  survives.
- The comic must be self-contained for a reader who sees the image before the
  caption; the caption supplies one portable principle and does not recap panels.
- Prefer a realistic four-beat causal chain: mandate, constraint, compliant attempt,
  institutional verdict. Narrow or broaden the seed only when truth, credibility,
  comprehension, and wit improve together.
- Preserve a monochrome newspaper-editorial register, stable humans, and the same
  silent GPT witness. Robot geometry and normal neutral eyes never drift between
  panels; its gaze may change, its model and emotional register may not.
- Use red only to trace the causal failure state. Avoid colourful cartoons,
  presentation-style infographics, named phenomena, generic paradox labels,
  unexplained objects, and comic dialogue that depends on the LinkedIn caption.
- Inspect the exact production image at full size and feed size. Before scheduling,
  the exact image plus caption must pass the five-row Design of Everyday Things
  diagnostic at 10/10.

## Adversarial repair addendum

Fresh verification found that three older generic rules could outrank the new
comic system. v0.9.3 therefore also repairs these before release:

- the 200–350 word target explicitly exempts the principle-only `State of Humans`
  caption;
- the generic text-only visual fallback is forbidden for comic-first episodes,
  because the image is the primary editorial artifact;
- God Mode and the publishing reference bind image delivery to the owner-bound
  `linkedin-cloudflare` route and require read-back of alt text, filename metadata,
  MIME type, bytes and hash before scheduling;
- the old ASCII-only rule is retained as legacy failure evidence and a recovery
  fallback, not as permission to mutate exact approved Unicode copy in the current
  cloud path;
- wiki filename, alt-text, weekday-review and cloud-capability language is
  reconciled to one current contract.
