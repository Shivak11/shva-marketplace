# SHVA LinkedIn Dual-Lane Editorial System v0.9.2

Date: 2026-08-29
Status: implementation authority

## Objective

Update `shva-linkedin-post-writer` so every serious or education LinkedIn topic can be developed through two deliberate candidates:

1. **Attention candidate** — optimised for immediate relevance, See-more entry, recognisable stakes, and qualified conversation.
2. **Authorship candidate** — optimised for Shiva's durable taste: connected cadence, category correction, ordinary work texture, and an earned systems reframe.

The two candidates must share one evidence-backed thesis. The attention candidate may compress or reorder the reveal; it may not use unsupported sensationalism, engagement bait, AI doom, or a generic creator voice.

## Locked Decisions

- Preserve one wiki identity per topic. Store both candidates and the selection/outcome ledger inside the same post record; do not create `-v1`, `-viral`, or `-taste` duplicate pages.
- Recommend one candidate before publication and record why. Preserve the alternate even when it is not selected.
- Add a post-level `editorial_lane` and `portfolio_genre`, plus a structured candidate comparison and post-publication outcome ledger.
- Judge performance through qualified signals: saves, substantive comments from the intended audience, profile views, site visits, training inquiries, and relevant invitations. Impressions are context, not the objective.
- Use a 12-published-post benchmark before changing genre slots. Compare within genre where possible; do not infer causality from one post.
- Use four portfolio genres with cold-start India times:
  - Monday 18:00 — personal taste / wider systems thinking.
  - Wednesday 16:00 — executive enterprise AI / honcho-attracting authority.
  - Thursday 16:30 — AI, work, and education.
  - Friday 15:30 — State of Humans editorial comic.
- These times are proposals for approval workflows, never unattended publication authority.
- Every unattended automation remains research/draft-only. No LinkedIn draft save, scheduling, publication, reaction, comment, or deletion without explicit later approval.
- Visuals must add an attention or decision function. Never attach an infographic merely to summarize the prose.
- Genre visual routes:
  - Executive: premium editorial evidence surface or data-art that a leader could take into a meeting; one contradiction, one decision question, restrained palette, no deck-like framework.
  - Education: a legible human/work transition or evidence surface that exposes the learning mechanism; avoid classroom stock imagery and generic future-of-work art.
  - Personal taste: art-led lateral image, surreal/absurdist editorial scene, or photographic treatment; usually little or no text.
  - Comic: established monochrome `State of Humans` system with stable cast and standalone comprehension.
- Do not imitate a named living artist. Translate useful properties such as hierarchy, negative space, restraint, data density, or editorial tension.
